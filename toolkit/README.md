# The Konsker Medical AI Toolkit

> Five workflows that turn AI from a chatbot into a study system.

These are the tools I use to study medicine at Stanford. They are free to use, modify, and share.

## The five workflows

### 1. [Lecture Distiller](01-lecture-distiller.md) — Custom Claude Skill

Turn 120 disorganized slides into 4 cleanly organized sections: pathophysiology, clinical presentation, differential diagnosis, clinical correlations.
**Time saved:** 3 hours → 5 minutes per lecture.

### 2. [Mnemonic Image Generator](02-mnemonic-image-generator.md) — Custom Claude + Gemini Workflow

Turn a hard-to-remember medical term into a single visual scene that encodes both the term's name and its meaning. I've made 140 of these this year.

### 3. [Anki Card Auto-Maker](03-anki-card-maker.md) — Custom Claude Skill

Upload a lecture slide, get back finished Anki cards in perfect format. The bottleneck of making cards is gone.
**Time saved:** 12 hours → 10 minutes per lecture.

### 4. [Lecture-as-Podcast](04-notebooklm-podcast.md) — NotebookLM Workflow

Turn any lecture into a 15-minute conversational podcast you can listen to on the way to class. Interactive mode lets you interrupt the hosts with questions.

### 5. [Lecture-to-Diagram](05-diagram-generator.md) — ChatGPT Workflow

When the lecture doesn't click, generate a visual diagram organized by the most important categories of the content.

## The philosophy

> *Using AI as a search engine gives you an answer.*
>
> *Using AI as a workflow gives you a personalized and more efficient way to learn.*

These tools were built individually, after class, by trial and error. That should not be the future of medical education. The next investment in AI medical education should be **better learning workflows** — built by the school, with students, for every student.

Until then, here are mine.
